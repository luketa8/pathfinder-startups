One ecosystem. Two ways to engage.

Whether a startup is looking to make the right connections, validate an opportunity, build an enterprise solution, or explore strategic investment, HPE provides a focused path forward.

Waypoint

Connect and validate with HPE

Waypoint gives promising startups a direct path to explore strategic, technical, and commercial opportunities with HPE.

Explore Waypoint

What Waypoint offers

  • Connect with the right teams – Reach HPE experts and business leaders relevant to your technology and market.
  • Get focused feedback – Gain technical, product, and commercial perspectives to help assess enterprise fit.
  • Explore opportunities – Identify potential proofs of concept, integrations, partnerships, and customer use cases.
  • Define the next step – Determine the most appropriate path forward based on mutual strategic fit.

Pathfinder

Invest, build, and scale

Hewlett Packard Pathfinder identifies and invests in category-leading startups, combines portfolio-company technologies with HPE offerings, and develops joint go-to-market programs.

Explore Pathfinder

What Pathfinder offers

  • Strategic investment – Investment in disruptive companies aligned with HPE’s strategic priorities.
  • Portfolio integration – Opportunities to combine startup technology with HPE products and platforms.
  • Validated solutions – Joint development of differentiated enterprise solutions.
  • Go-to-market acceleration – Programs that bring HPE and startup capabilities to enterprise customers together.

Which path is right for you?

Start with what your company needs

Choose Waypoint if you want to

  • Introduce your technology to relevant HPE teams
  • Receive focused technical or commercial feedback
  • Explore a proof of concept or integration
  • Determine whether there is a strategic fit
  • Establish an initial relationship with HPE

Explore Pathfinder if you want to

  • Pursue a strategic investment relationship
  • Integrate your technology with the HPE portfolio
  • Create a validated enterprise solution
  • Build a joint go-to-market opportunity
  • Develop a deeper strategic relationship with HPE
